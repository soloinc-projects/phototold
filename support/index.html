Support

Write to support@8086.llc. Phototold is made by 8086 LLC, and a person reads every message. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing. Please don't send recordings: we never need them to help.

Questions people ask first

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Phototold keeps everything on your phone.

What is free, and what does the unlock add?

Phototold is free to download, and the first chapter is free: record it, play it back and save it as a film. One in-app purchase, $14.99 once in the U.S., unlocks every chapter after the first in every session, the film of the whole session, and transcripts and subtitle files for every chapter. There is no subscription and nothing renews. The store shows the price where you live.

How do I get the unlock back on a new phone?

Tap Restore purchase, on the unlock screen or in About. Use the same Apple Account on iPhone, or the same Google account on Android, as when you bought it. Restoring needs a connection to the store.

Where is the film?

Make the film saves one film, with a chapter for each photo, to your Photos library on iPhone, or to Movies on Android, where your gallery shows it. Cancelling leaves nothing behind. The subtitle files and the transcript come with it.

A call came in, or the phone died, in the middle of a take. Is it lost?

What was recorded is kept, and the photo stays in the queue. Open the session: it says where the chapter was cut short, and you can carry on with it or keep it as it is.

It says it needs the camera or the microphone.

Phototold uses the camera and microphone only to record a chapter. If you said no when the phone asked, tap Open Settings on that message and turn them on for Phototold. If another app is using the camera, close it and come back.

Some of my photos aren't there (iPhone).

If you let Phototold see only selected photos, it shows those. Add more from the photo picker in Phototold, or allow more photos in Settings, then Phototold, then Photos.

Does it work without a connection?

Recording, playback and making the film all work in Airplane Mode. Buying and restoring the unlock go through the App Store or Google Play.

Why does my phone use one screen for recording?

On a phone with a second, outer screen, the photo can face the storyteller on the outer screen while you hold the inner one. Every other phone uses one screen, with the photo at the top nearest the camera. About, then Recording setup, says which one your phone uses.

How do I delete a chapter, a session or everything?

Open a chapter and tap Delete this chapter. Delete a session from the library. To delete everything Phototold made, go to About, then Delete all recordings; it asks twice. Deleting never touches your own photos.

Is Phototold a therapy or memory-care app?

No. Phototold records stories. It makes no health claim, it is not a medical device, and it is not a substitute for care from a clinician.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple. On Android: Google Play refund policies.

Is it accessible?

Phototold is designed to WCAG 2.2 AA, with the largest text sizes, VoiceOver on iPhone and TalkBack on Android, dark mode, and reduced motion. If something is hard to use, please write to us.
