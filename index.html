Their stories on video, one family photo at a time.

Pick the family photos you want to ask about. For each one, Phototold shows the question that photo raises. Your parent looks at the photo and answers, and the answer is kept on video as a chapter beside it.

Phototold records a parent's or grandparent's stories in video, one family photo and one chapter at a time, on your phone: no account, nothing uploaded, not a therapy app and not a printed book.

  • App Store: coming soon for iPhone
  • Google Play: coming soon for Android phones

No account, no ads, no tracking: the stories stay on your phone. Privacy policy

The photo does the asking

Each photo comes with a question written for what a family photo can show: who is in it, where it was taken, what that day was like. The photo sits at the top of the screen, nearest the camera, so the storyteller looks at it while they talk, with the question under it, a small preview, the timer and Stop.

Before each take you name the person and tap once: "Dad agrees · Start recording". That one tap records their agreement and starts recording. Tap Next photo to go on to the next chapter without stopping.

Kept on your phone the moment you tap Stop

Each answer is a numbered chapter with its photo, its question, its length and the date. It is saved on the phone the moment you tap Stop. If a take is cut short by a call, a closed app or a flat battery, what was recorded is kept and the photo stays in the queue.

There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your photos stay in your library, and Phototold only reads the ones you choose. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and Google Play's Data safety reads no data collected, no data shared.

One film, with a chapter for each photo

Tap Make the film and the whole session is saved as one film, with a chapter for each photo: to Photos on iPhone, to Movies on Android. You also get a subtitle file for each chapter and the whole transcript as text.

Each chapter gets a transcript, made on the phone, and you can search what was said across every session.

Two screens, where the phone has them

On a phone with a second, outer screen, the photo shows whole on the outer screen, facing the storyteller beside the rear camera. The question, a live sound level, the timer and Stop stay on the inner screen in your hands. On every other phone, one screen does it all.

Free to start, then one price

Phototold is free to download, and the first chapter is free: record it, play it back and save it as a film. One in-app purchase, $14.99 once in the U.S., unlocks every chapter after the first in every session, the film of the whole session, and transcripts and subtitle files for every chapter. No subscription, no trial, nothing that renews. The store shows the price where you live, and Restore purchase brings it back on a new phone.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants a parent's or grandparent's stories kept in their own face and voice, using the family photos already on the phone, with no account and nothing uploaded.

The questions are written by the company, in English, and come with the app. Phototold has no AI voice and no AI interviewer: it keeps the real person.

What it is not

  • Not a printed book: it makes a film.
  • Not a therapy or memory-care app, and it makes no health claim.
  • Not a cloud service: Phototold uploads nothing, and shares a chapter or a film only when you tap Share.

Designed to WCAG 2.2 AA: the largest text sizes, VoiceOver and TalkBack, dark mode, and reduced motion.
