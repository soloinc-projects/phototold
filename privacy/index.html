Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. This policy covers the Phototold app on iPhone and Android and these pages.

In short: Phototold collects no data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code in the app, and the app makes no network request of its own. Your recordings, transcripts and photos stay on your phone. On the App Store its privacy label is Data Not Collected; on Google Play its Data safety reads no data collected and no data shared.

Phototold is made by 8086 LLC ("we"). Questions about this policy: support@8086.llc.

What Phototold keeps on your phone

These stay inside the app on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

  • Recordings: the video and sound of each chapter you record.
  • Transcripts: the words of each chapter, written on the phone by the phone's own speech recognition.
  • Chapter details: which photo the chapter is about, the question asked, its length and date, the storyteller's name as you typed it on the consent card, and the time they agreed.
  • A small still of each recorded photo, so a chapter still shows its photo if you later remove the original from your library.
  • Your sessions: their names and the order of their photos.
  • Your purchase: whether the unlock has been bought.
  • On iPhone, the phone's own search: each kept chapter is added to Spotlight on your phone (its question, date and session), so you can find it from the Home Screen.
  • A crash report file: if the app crashes, a short report is written inside the app on your phone. It is never sent anywhere.
  • An event log: a record of app events on the phone, such as a chapter started or a film saved, used to check that the app works. It holds no names, photos, transcripts or identifiers, and it is never sent anywhere.

Your photos, the camera and the microphone

  • Photos: Phototold reads only the photos you choose. You pick photos with the phone's own photo picker, which needs no access to your library. On iPhone, Phototold asks to add to Photos when you first save a film, and asks for access to your library only when you delete a session or every recording, to remove the films it saved; iPhone asks you to confirm. Phototold never edits, deletes or uploads your own photos.
  • Camera and microphone: used only to record a chapter, after you start a take. The phone asks for permission the first time.
  • The film: when you tap Make the film, the film is saved to your Photos library on iPhone or to Movies on Android. From then on it is in your own library, under your control.
  • Sharing: a chapter or a film leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where it goes, in your phone's own share sheet.

The people you record

Before every take, Phototold shows a consent card naming the person being recorded, and nothing records until it is tapped. The name you type and the time of that agreement are kept with the chapter, on your phone. Please record only people who have agreed. You can delete a chapter at any time.

The App Store and Google Play

  • Buying and restoring the unlock goes through the App Store or Google Play, under Apple's or Google's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details. The app checks your purchase with the store on the phone.
  • Age signal: where a law requires it, Phototold asks the store for your age range at first launch (or at the next launch, if the store did not answer), and never again after it answers (Apple's Declared Age Range, or Google Play's age signals). Nothing in the app depends on your age, so the answer changes nothing. The app keeps only that the signal was read and which range came back, on your phone.
  • Rating: after a chapter is kept or a film is saved, the app may ask the store to show its own rating prompt.
  • Crash reports: if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may send us their own crash reports. Phototold adds nothing to them.

Your phone's own backups

On iPhone, if your phone backs itself up to iCloud, that backup can include what Phototold keeps; it is yours, under your Apple Account and Apple's terms. On Android, Phototold leaves everything out of Google's cloud backup; a phone-to-phone transfer to your new phone can carry your sessions. Phototold itself uploads nothing.

Deleting

  • A chapter: open it and tap Delete this chapter. Its video and transcript are deleted from the phone.
  • A session: delete it from the library. Its chapters and Phototold's own copies are deleted, and Phototold also removes the films it saved for that session (on iPhone after you allow it and confirm).
  • Everything: About, then Delete all recordings. It asks twice, then deletes every chapter and transcript kept in Phototold and the films it saved.
  • Removing the app deletes everything kept inside it.
  • Saved films: deleting a single chapter leaves a film already saved in Photos or Movies as it is; delete that film there, or delete the session. If you refuse Phototold access on iPhone, the film stays in Photos and the app tells you how to remove it.

Deleting in Phototold never touches your own photos. There is no account, so there is nothing for us to delete: we hold no data about you.

Children

Phototold is made for adults and is not directed at children. Children may appear in your family's photos and films, which stay on your phone. We collect no data from anyone, including children.

These pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, analytics or tracking of our own. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date. If Phototold ever starts collecting data, the app's store privacy answers change first, and this page says what, why and how to delete it.
